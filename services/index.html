Marla Garden develops bespoke steel and glass conservatories, pavilions and gazebos for London homes and gardens.

We discuss the setting and intended use, then agree the design, fabrication and installation scope for your structure.

How your project takes shape

Project brief and site review

We start with how you want to use the space, photographs of the property and approximate dimensions. We discuss access, the existing building and what needs to be checked on site.

Design and drawings

As part of an agreed structure project, we develop its layout, elevations and glazing. Where included in the scope, drawings and renders help you review the proposal before fabrication.

See how we use drawings and renders ↗

Steel fabrication and glazing

Once the design and scope are agreed, the frame, metalwork and glazing are coordinated around the confirmed openings, finish and roof form.

Installation and handover

We agree how the structure will be delivered and installed, what needs to be ready on site and who is responsible for enabling works and interfaces.
