The design

A long glazed veranda runs beside a masonry house, with fine frame divisions, arched lights and cresting along the roof. Interior lighting picks out the frame in a winter garden.

It emphasises the relationship between a long glazed structure and the existing house.

Where it works

Large houses with a long garden elevation suit this veranda. The winter scene is a setting, not evidence of heating or weather performance.

Making it yours
