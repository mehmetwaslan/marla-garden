The architectural character

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

The reference explores the transition between an existing façade, a covered edge and terrace seating. Any commercial use, enclosure and structural requirements need to be established separately for the proposed site.

The design

A tall glazed veranda runs along the face of a pale building, with pointed Gothic arches and fine glazing bars repeated down its length. Ornamental details at the roof edge give the long elevation its rhythm.

Because it is attached, the veranda borrows the height of the existing façade and turns a terrace into a covered, glazed edge.

Where it works

Townhouses and country houses with a terrace along one side suit a veranda. It creates a sheltered threshold between house and garden, ideal for dining. Any commercial use and the structural connection must be assessed for the specific building.
