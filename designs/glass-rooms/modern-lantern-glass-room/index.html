The design

A broad glazed room with a raised roof lantern, shown in dark and green finishes. The lantern lifts light into the centre of a deep plan.

Comparing finishes shows how colour changes the character of the same structure.

Where it works

Larger gardens and terraces suit this room, as a dining or living space.

Making it yours

Lantern, plan and finish are set per project. See our steel and glass conservatories, the Black Lantern Room, and the lantern section in our design studio.
