The design

A small extension against the house, with a glazed roof and dark framing in a simple grid. It adds daylight and a garden connection without a large footprint.

The dark frame keeps the outline crisp against the existing wall.

Where it works

Side returns, small rear gardens and kitchen extensions suit a compact glass room.

Making it yours

Size, roof pitch and openings are designed for the house. See our steel and glass conservatories, the Terrace Glass Room, and the London planning guide.
