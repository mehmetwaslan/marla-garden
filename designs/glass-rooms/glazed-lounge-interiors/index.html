The design

These concepts show the inside of a long glazed lounge: exposed dark steel roof framing, decorative upper glazing, chequered floors, fireplaces and generous seating. They explore how a glass room can be furnished as a real living space.

Exterior views show the same room beside a house in winter.

Where it works

A large glazed extension or garden room used for living and entertaining suits this kind of interior planning.
