Dark Slim-Frame Glass Rooms

Dark straight frames and a pitched glazed roof create a quieter garden-room direction. Broad elevations and clear glazing place the emphasis on the interior and the surrounding garden.

The design

This is the quietest direction in the collection: slim dark frames, a pitched glazed roof and large clear panes, with almost no ornament. The structure draws a precise outline and then steps back so the interior and the garden take over.

A deep fascia at the eaves gives the roof a defined edge, and the corners are kept as slim as the glazing allows.

Where it works

Modern and mid-century houses, and gardens with strong contemporary landscaping, suit a dark slim frame. It also works beside period houses where a clearly modern addition is preferred to a reproduction style.
