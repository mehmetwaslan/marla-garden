Choosing your direction

Consider the connection to the house.

Quiet dark frame or pale pitched form?

Dark straight framing emphasises the outline of the glazing; a pale frame reads differently against sky and masonry. Compare the roof shape and window divisions as well as the finish.

Dark Slim-Frame Glass Rooms ↗White Pitched Glass Room ↗

Attached room or independent pavilion?

An attached room needs a considered junction with the existing façade and a route through the house. A separate pavilion has its own entrance and garden approach. Establish which relationship suits the intended use.
