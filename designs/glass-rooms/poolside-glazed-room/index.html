The architectural character

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

The relationship to the terrace is central to this reference. The apparent poolside setting is part of the design image; it does not establish suitability for a humid pool enclosure without a separate specification.

The design

A long, low glazed room runs along a pool terrace, with arched lights and a restrained roof edge giving the elevation a horizontal emphasis. Interior light glows through the panels at dusk.

The relationship to the water and terrace is the heart of the design.

Where it works

A garden with a pool or a long terrace suits this room. A humid pool enclosure needs its own specification — the image shows the setting, not that suitability.

Making it yours
