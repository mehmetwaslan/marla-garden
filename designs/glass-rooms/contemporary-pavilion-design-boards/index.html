The design

These boards explore one direction from several viewpoints: dark-framed pavilions with broad glazed openings, shown from the garden, the terrace and inside. Corner glazing, roof form and room layout can be compared side by side.

Each board is a design presentation, not a record of a completed installation.

Where it works

Contemporary gardens and terraces suit this direction, as a garden office, dining room or second living space.

Making it yours

Layout, glazing and roof form are developed from your brief. See our steel and glass conservatories, compare dark slim-frame glass rooms, or the Black Lantern Room.
