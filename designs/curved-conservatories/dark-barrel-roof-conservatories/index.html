Compare the end of the curved roof, the depth of the eaves and the entrance arrangement. The evening views explore the relationship between the frame and interior lighting.

The design

A continuous curved glazed roof sits over a dark rectangular conservatory, with tall window divisions and decorative arched panels below the eaves. The long elevation has an ordered, almost industrial rhythm.

Evening views show how the dark frame contrasts with warm interior light.

Where it works

Contemporary and period houses alike suit a dark barrel roof; it reads as a confident modern take on the Victorian curved conservatory.

Making it yours
