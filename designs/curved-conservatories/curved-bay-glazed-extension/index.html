Curved-Bay Glazed Extension

A pale glazed extension with rounded bays and closely spaced vertical glazing. The design board brings the entrance and curved architectural details together.

The architectural character

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

A projecting curved bay changes both the interior plan and the view from the garden. These reference views help compare the radius, glazing rhythm and roof-edge relationship.

The design

Rounded bays bring the curve into the plan as well as the roof. Closely spaced vertical glazing bars follow the radius, and a curved roof edge with ornamental detail caps the elevation.

The design board brings together the full view, the entrance and close-ups of the curved details.

Where it works

A house with a garden-facing elevation and room for a projecting bay suits this extension. The curve changes both the interior plan and the view from the garden.

Making it yours

Bay radius, glazing rhythm and roof-edge detail are set per project. Explore classical conservatories, see the White Bay Conservatory and Terracotta Glass Extension projects.
