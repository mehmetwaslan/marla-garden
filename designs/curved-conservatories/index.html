Choosing your direction

Follow the curve through roof and elevation.

Pale or dark barrel roof?

Both references use a continuous roof curve above a long glazed frontage. Compare the glazing rhythm, entrance and decorative upper panels; the frame finish changes how clearly those lines stand out.

Cream Barrel-Roof Conservatories ↗Dark Barrel-Roof Conservatories ↗

A curved roof or rounded bays?

A barrel roof curves over the room. Rounded bays bring the curve into the plan and elevation. Review the footprint and the connection to the house before treating these as interchangeable design choices.
