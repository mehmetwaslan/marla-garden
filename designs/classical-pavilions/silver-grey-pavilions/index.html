The architectural character

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

Grey can be considered alongside the existing masonry, paths and planting. These views are useful for comparing the scale of the glazing and the balance between the doorway and side panels.

The design

A restrained grey frame under a glazed roof, with tall window panels, ornamental base panels and a projecting entrance. The muted colour lets the roof shape and arch patterns carry the design.

Grey sits between the softness of cream and the contrast of black.

Where it works

Grey suits gardens with stone paving, slate or weathered timber, and houses in neutral tones.

Making it yours

Exact shade, roof form and glazing scale are chosen for the setting. See our glass garden pavilions, compare dark gabled pavilions, or read the cost guide.
