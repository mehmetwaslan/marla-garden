Cream Pitched-Roof Pavilions

Pale frames and repeated arched glazing give these pitched-roof pavilions a classical garden character. Steps and a defined entrance create a clear approach through the planting.

The architectural character

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

Look at the relationship between the roof pitch, entrance bay and height of the windows. The light finish makes the arch patterns particularly legible against the trees and glass.

The design

A pitched glazed roof over a pale ornamental frame, with repeated arched lights and a decorative base. An entrance bay with its own small gable projects at the front, reached by steps.

The cream finish makes the arch patterns especially legible against glass, trees and sky.

Where it works

A freestanding pavilion like this suits a lawn or terrace set away from the house, where it can be seen from several sides.

Making it yours

Roof pitch, entrance bay and window height are set per project. See our glass garden pavilions, the Ivory Arch Pavilion, or compare curved-roof pavilions.
