The architectural character

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

The balance of roof detail, window rhythm and doorway gives this reference its character. Compare the direct front view with the wider setting before selecting the elements for your own brief.

The design

A dark frame with decorative arched panels, a glazed hipped roof and a small lantern on top. Curtains inside and urns at the steps give the room a furnished, salon-like feel.

The balance of roof detail, window rhythm and doorway gives it its character.

Where it works

A planted approach in a formal garden suits this room. It works as a dining room, garden salon or quiet retreat.

Making it yours

Ornament, colour and size are chosen per project. See our glass garden pavilions, the Crest Garden Salon, or compare the Lantern-Roof Garden Salon.
