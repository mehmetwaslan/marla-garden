The design

A circular plan gives this pavilion a balanced silhouette from every side. Slender columns carry a domed roof, with decorative railings around a raised platform.

Because it is round, the pavilion is approached and viewed differently from each direction.

Where it works

A lawn, a garden crossing or a spot with views all around suits a circular pavilion. It can be left open or enclosed with glazing.

Making it yours

Diameter, enclosure and ornament are set per project. See our glass garden pavilions and metal gazebos, or compare the Circular Domed Garden Room.
