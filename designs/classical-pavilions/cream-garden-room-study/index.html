The design

A study of one pavilion from several angles: the main exterior view, a smaller lantern roof study and the furnished interior. Warm evening light shows how the frame reads after dark.

Ornamental base panels and a lantern roof give it a classical outline.

Where it works

A lawn or terrace with views from the house suits this pavilion, as a dining room or garden room.
