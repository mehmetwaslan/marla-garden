The design

A group of domed pavilions with curtains and lanterns, shown at night on an open terrace. Each pavilion forms its own room within a larger setting.

Repeating one pavilion design creates a coherent hospitality space.

Where it works

Hotels, venues and estates suit a group of pavilions. Commercial use requires its own specification and approvals.

Making it yours

Number, size and finish are developed per brief. See our glass garden pavilions, compare circular pavilions, or discuss a hospitality project.
