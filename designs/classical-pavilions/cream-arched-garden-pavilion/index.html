Cream Arched Garden Pavilion

A pale ornamental garden pavilion with repeated arched glazing sits within mature planting. The entrance and roof details create a clear front elevation.

The architectural character

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

The garden approach frames the pavilion as a destination. Consider the height of the windows, entrance scale and planting around the base when comparing this direction with your own setting.

The design

A long, low pavilion in a pale cream finish, set at the end of a stone path with steps rising to the entrance. Repeated arched lights run the full length of the elevation, and a shallow glazed roof keeps the outline calm so the arches carry the character.

The proportions are those of a classical orangery rather than a small summerhouse: a broad frontage, a modest height and a strong horizontal line. The pale frame reads softly against hedging and mature trees.

Where it works

A long garden with a clear axis from the house suits this design best — the pavilion becomes the end point of the view. It also works along a boundary wall, where its length can screen a neighbouring property without feeling heavy.

Making it yours

Length, bay spacing and frame colour are set for each garden. Compare it with our Ivory Arch Pavilion concept and the wider range of glass garden pavilions, then see how plans and elevations fix the proportions in our design studio.
