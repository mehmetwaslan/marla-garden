The architectural character

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

The reference brings together roof height, entrance detail and an intimate garden setting. Compare those elements with the room size and furniture arrangement you have in mind.

The design

A green salon with a raised lantern above a hipped roof, curtains inside and an ornamental entrance at the top of lit steps. Warm interior light contrasts with the cooler roof and trees.

Roof height, entrance detail and an intimate setting come together in one composition.

Where it works

A wooded or well-planted garden suits this salon, as a dining room, study or evening retreat.

Making it yours

Room size, lantern and furniture layout are agreed per brief. See our glass garden pavilions, compare the Decorative Garden Room, or the Black Lantern Room.
