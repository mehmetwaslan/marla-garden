The architectural character

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

The lantern changes the silhouette from a simple pitched room. Compare the transition between the main roof and the upper feature, together with the entrance width and the view through the glazing.

The design

A raised lantern lifts the centre of the roof into a second glazed tier, giving the glasshouse a stepped Victorian silhouette. Below it, a rich green frame, arched glazing and a central doorway at the top of a short flight of steps make the building a destination.

The lantern is not only decorative: its upper glazing brings light and air into the middle of the space, where a single roof plane would be darkest.

Where it works

A walled garden, a formal parterre or the end of a straight path suits a symmetrical glasshouse like this. It can be used for plants, for sitting, or both — the brief decides the glazing and ventilation.
