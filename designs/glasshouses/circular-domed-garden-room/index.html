The design

A circular glazed room with a ribbed dome and a curved elevation facing the garden. The round plan gives the room equal views in every direction.

Curved ribs carry the dome to a central point.

Where it works

A lawn or terrace with open views suits a circular room, for dining or as a garden salon.

Making it yours

Diameter, dome and finish are set per project. See our glass garden pavilions, the Winter Dome Pavilion, or compare the Domed Botanical Glasshouses.
