The architectural character

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

Look at the relationship between the main glazing panels, the roof and the path to the entrance. The atmosphere of the reference can inform a brief while the technical construction is specified separately.

The design

A green glasshouse with a hipped glazed roof, cresting along the ridge and a central doorway framed by urns and planting. Warm light inside shows the glazing pattern from the garden.

The proportions are compact and formal, with the entrance on the main axis and symmetrical glazing either side.

Where it works

A courtyard, a walled garden or a formal planting scheme suits this glasshouse. It can house plants, a seating area or a mix of both.

Making it yours

Plan size, roof form and frame colour are set per project. See our glass garden pavilions, compare the Green Lantern-Roof Glasshouses, or the Green Gable Conservatory project.
