Choosing your direction

Start with the roof and the purpose.

Lantern or dome?

A raised lantern creates a layered roofline. A dome carries its curved ribs across the space. Look at both the exterior silhouette and the interior view upwards.

Green Lantern-Roof Glasshouses ↗Domed Botanical Glasshouses ↗

Compact destination or broader garden room?

Compare a compact pavilion with a broader pitched-roof form. Decide what needs to fit inside and how people will move around it. A glasshouse appearance does not establish a growing environment or year-round comfort specification.
