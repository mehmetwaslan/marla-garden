The design

A raised roof lantern sits above a broad, pale glasshouse with long glazed elevations. The repeated window pattern gives it an open, airy character.

The lantern adds a second level of light at the centre of the roof.

Where it works

A wide terrace or lawn where the long elevation can be seen suits this design. It works as a garden room or a light-filled space for plants.

Making it yours

Length, lantern size and door alignment are set per project. See our glass garden pavilions, compare green lantern glasshouses, or browse garden glasshouse designs.
