Domed Botanical Glasshouses

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

The design boards make the relationship between dome, entrance and surrounding landscape easier to compare. Intended use, environmental conditions and final construction require their own project brief.

The design

A glazed dome on curved ribs rises above tall framing, in the tradition of the great Victorian palm houses. The boards show the dome from outside, inside and from a distance.

The dome brings light down into the whole space and gives the building a landmark silhouette.

Where it works

Larger gardens and estates suit a domed glasshouse, for plants, gathering or both. Intended use and environmental conditions need their own brief.

Making it yours

Dome size, drum height and finish are set per project. See our glass garden pavilions, the Winter Dome Pavilion, or the Twin Dome Conservatory.
