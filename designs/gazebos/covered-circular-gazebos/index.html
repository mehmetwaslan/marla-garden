Compare the low canopy, raised roof cap and different frame finishes in these design references. The open enclosure gives the seating area a different relationship to the landscape from a glazed room.

The design

A shaped canopy on slender columns covers a circular seating area on a raised platform. The sides stay open, so the gazebo defines a room without enclosing it.

A decorative perimeter rail and a small raised roof cap give the outline its detail; the canopy itself is kept simple.

Where it works

A covered gazebo suits a lawn, terrace or poolside where you want shade and a defined seating area. It is more sheltered than an open lattice dome, but it is not a glazed room.

Making it yours

Diameter, canopy material and frame finish are specified for each garden. See our ornamental metal gazebos, compare curtained garden gazebos, or consider an enclosed glass garden pavilion.
