Curtained Garden Gazebos

The architectural character

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

Curtains change the enclosure and the way the structure is experienced from the garden. Their appearance in these references does not establish weather resistance; canopy construction and fabric requirements belong in the project specification.

The design

Light fabric panels hang between the columns of a covered circular gazebo, softening its edge and creating a room within the garden. The bays can be left open or drawn closed.

The roof is a simple canopy; the curtains do the work of defining the space.

Where it works

A curtained gazebo suits outdoor dining on a terrace or lawn, where shade and a sense of enclosure matter more than glazing. Fabric is not a weatherproof wall — that belongs in the specification.

Making it yours

Diameter, fabric and frame finish are chosen for the garden. See our ornamental metal gazebos, compare covered circular gazebos, and view the curtained gazebo drawing in our design studio.
