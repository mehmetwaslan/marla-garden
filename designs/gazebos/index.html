Choosing your direction

Decide how open the garden space should feel.

Open lattice or continuous canopy?

An open lattice dome frames the sky and planting through decorative ribs. A covered canopy changes the character of the space and the shelter you want to discuss. The image alone does not confirm a weatherproof specification.

Open sides or fabric panels?

Open bays keep the garden visible around the seating. Curtains soften the edge and can change the sense of enclosure. Compare the entrance, furniture arrangement and views from the seats.
