Open Lattice Dome Gazebos

An open ornamental dome gives this family its silhouette. Curved ribs and decorative scrollwork create a roof outline through which the garden remains visible.

The architectural character

LOOK CLOSER AT THE DETAILS.

The lattice is an architectural feature rather than a continuous weatherproof covering. Compare the density of the ornamental work, the entrance opening and the shape of the dome when preparing a design brief.

The design

The dome is the whole idea: curved ribs rise from a ring of slender columns and meet at a crowning finial, with dense scrollwork filling the spaces between them. Because the lattice is open, the dome frames the sky rather than closing it off.

Lower panels of matching scrollwork can enclose part of the perimeter, creating a sense of a room while keeping the garden in view.

Where it works

An open gazebo suits a lawn, the end of a path or a paved terrace where you want a decorative focal point and a place to sit. It is a garden ornament first and a shelter second — the lattice is not a weatherproof roof.

Making it yours

Column circle, dome height, density of ornament and finish are all decided for the setting. See our ornamental metal garden gazebos, the Green Filigree Gazebo concept, or compare a covered circular gazebo.
