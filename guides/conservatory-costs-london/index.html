Bespoke Conservatory Costs in London

Home/Journal/Bespoke Conservatory Costs in London

A useful conservatory budget begins with a defined project. Two structures with the same footprint can have very different costs when their frame, glazing, decorative work and site requirements differ.

Start with the whole project

Separate the glazed structure from the work needed to make it usable. Groundworks, foundations, drainage, alterations to the house, electrical work, heating and finishes may sit outside a structure-only quotation. Ask each supplier to show what is included and who is responsible for the remaining work.

The form and the details

A curved façade, a vaulted entrance, bespoke arches and ornamental cresting introduce different fabrication and glazing requirements from a straight, repetitive frame. Choose the architectural features that matter most to you before comparing prices.

Glazing and comfort

Discuss orientation, glare, summer heat, ventilation and winter use together. Ask for the proposed glazing and frame specification in writing. A photograph cannot establish thermal performance, and a decorative appearance does not tell you whether two quotations specify equivalent construction.

Access and the existing house

For a London project, access through the house, restricted delivery space and the connection to the existing elevation can influence the proposed method and scope. Share photographs of the access route as well as the garden. Note any known drainage, trees, level changes or existing structures in the proposed footprint.

Compare quotations on the same basis

Request a breakdown covering the structure, glazing, finishes, delivery, installation and site works. Confirm VAT, exclusions, payment stages, programme assumptions, warranty terms and aftercare. If one quotation is substantially lower, identify the difference in scope before judging value.

Prepare a useful first brief

Send the postcode, photographs, an approximate width and depth, your intended use and a few design references. Add any drawings already available. A first conversation can then focus on the architectural direction and the information needed for a project-specific quotation.
