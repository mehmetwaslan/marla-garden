The project

The Black Lantern Room

The Black Lantern Room is a compact glazed garden room built around a single idea: a raised glazed lantern at the centre of a hipped roof, bringing daylight down into the middle of the space.

Dark steel framing and solid base panels give it a crisp, contemporary outline on a stone-paved base. The image is a garden design concept, developed further as a full design study.

Structure
Freestanding glazed garden room
Frame
Steel, dark finish, solid base panels
Roof
Glazed hipped roof at 22° with a raised glazed lantern
Footprint
6.0 × 3.0 m, 18 m² (from our design drawing)
Stage
Garden design concept and design study

Frame, roof and lantern

The hipped roof rises at 22° to the lantern, whose own glazed sides lift light and air above the main roof. Glazed double doors sit on the centre of the garden elevation, aligned with the lantern above.

Solid base panels give the room a defined plinth and some privacy at seated height, while the glazing above stays clear.
