The project

The Green Gable Conservatory

The Green Gable Conservatory is organised around one strong element: a glazed triangular gable that marks the entrance and lifts the centre of the façade.

Repeated fanlight patterns carry the same curve into the doors and side glazing, and the green frame sits comfortably among established trees and planted borders.

Frame, roof and glazing

The glazed pitched roof rises to the central gable, where the entrance doors open straight onto the garden path. Circular details within the fanlights repeat across the elevation, giving the glazing a consistent rhythm.

A green finish is a classic choice for garden structures because it recedes into planting. Here it lets the white light of the glazing and the shape of the gable stand out.

Design and build

Designing around an entrance

A gable entrance changes the plan as well as the elevation: it sets the main axis of the room and the route from the garden. In our design studio we show how plans and elevations are used to decide this early.
