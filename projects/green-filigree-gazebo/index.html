The project

The Green Filigree Gazebo

The Green Filigree Gazebo is an open garden structure: eight slender columns carry a lattice dome of curved ribs and scrollwork, above a low circular stone platform.

Because the roof is open, the gazebo frames the sky and surrounding planting rather than enclosing a room. This image is a garden design concept, not a photograph of a completed installation.

Structure
Open-sided gazebo on eight columns
Frame
Painted green ornamental metalwork
Roof
Open lattice dome with scrollwork
Footprint
3.6 m column circle on a 5.0 m platform (indicative, from our design drawing)
Stage
Garden design concept

Form and detail

The dome is built up from curved members that meet at a crowning finial, with scrollwork filling the spaces between them. Arched brackets link each column to the dome.

A raised platform defines the seating area and gives the gazebo a clear edge within the lawn.

Garden design concept.

Design and build

Designed in drawings

We drew this gazebo as an elevation, plan and section to establish the column circle, platform size and dome height. It is shown in our design studio alongside an enclosed pavilion and a curtained gazebo.
