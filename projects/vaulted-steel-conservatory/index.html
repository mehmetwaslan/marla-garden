The project

The Vaulted Steel Conservatory

This photograph was taken inside a long vaulted conservatory during construction. Before any glass is fitted, the repeated arched ribs set out the full length and curvature of the building.

We include it because it shows something a finished photograph cannot: the steel structure itself, and how the form is established before glazing.

Location
Istanbul, Türkiye (workshop yard assembly)
Year
2024
Structure
Long vaulted conservatory
Frame
Steel arched ribs with ornamental scrollwork
Stage
Construction — before glazing and finishing
Made by
Steelwork fabricated and installed by our partner ironwork atelier in Türkiye

What the frame shows

Each rib is an arch of steel, repeated at regular intervals to form the vault. Ornamental scrollwork follows the upper edge between the ribs — decoration that is fixed as part of the frame rather than added later.

Site equipment, ladders and open members are still visible. This is a record of work in progress, not a completed room.

Design and build

How the frame goes up

Our construction film follows the assembly from bare ground: base plates and column lines are set out on a levelled gravel bed, the uprights are bolted and braced, and the arched ribs are lifted in by crane and fixed one bay at a time. Joints are cut and dressed on site with grinders, while the ornamental scroll panels are offered up between the ribs from scaffold towers. Seeing the whole vault in bare steel makes it clear how much of the final building is decided before a single pane is fitted.
