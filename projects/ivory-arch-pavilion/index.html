The project

The Ivory Arch Pavilion

The Ivory Arch Pavilion is a pale freestanding pavilion at the end of a garden path. Repeated arched lights wrap around its corners, and wide steps lead up to a pair of open doors.

This image is a garden design concept rather than a photograph of a completed installation.

Form and detail

A cream frame gives the pavilion a soft outline against planting and sky. The arched glazing pattern continues around the corners without interruption, so the structure reads as one composition from the approach.

A shallow stone platform and steps raise the floor and frame the entrance.
