The project

The Crest Garden Salon

The Crest Garden Salon is a square glazed room on a terrace, framed in dark steel. Full-height glazed doors run between panelled posts, each topped by a pierced filigree panel cut as an arch, and a semicircular ornamental crest rises above the entrance between two lanterns.

The steel frame was fabricated and installed on site. The image here is a completion study based on that installed frame: it shows the finished glazing and a cleared terrace, rather than the site as it was on the day of installation.

The design

A deep fascia and cornice carry a shallow metal roof, so the outline stays crisp and the crest reads clearly against the sky. Below the fascia, a band of fretwork panels ties the bays together.

The arch panels give each bay a softer head without curving the glass itself, which keeps the glazing simple to make and replace. The flush glazed doors fold back so the salon can open fully to the terrace.
