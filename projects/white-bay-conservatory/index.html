The White Bay Conservatory

The White Bay Conservatory draws on the Victorian taste for coloured glass. A rounded glazed bay projects from the house, and a band of coloured panes runs above the main windows, catching the light differently through the day.

White framing keeps the structure light against the pale pink house, while a brick plinth anchors it to the garden.

Frame and glazing

The glazing follows a clear rhythm of rectangular panes with diamond-shaped details. The coloured panels sit only in the upper band, so views from inside to the garden stay clear while the conservatory gains its character from above.

The brick plinth and the white roof edge tie the lower and upper parts of the elevation together, giving the rounded bay a defined base and top.
