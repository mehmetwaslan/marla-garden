The project

The Terracotta Glass Extension

The Terracotta Glass Extension runs along the garden side of the house as a single curved glazed volume. Its roof springs from just below the eaves and curves down into rounded end bays, so the extension has no hard corners at all.

A bronze steel frame sits against the warm terracotta render, and two outward-opening vents break the regular grid of small panes.

Frame, roof and glazing

Where the curved roof meets the house it is flashed to the wall, and the room opens from the house along its length. Small-pane glazing keeps the scale domestic, while the rounded corners continue the curve of the roof into the plan.

A panelled base on a brick plinth lifts the glazing off the ground and ties the new structure to the materials of the existing house.

Design and build

From drawing to finished structure

Our design-record drawing shows the garden elevation, a section through the roof abutment and the plan with its opening to the house. It is one of the examples in our design studio of how a glass extension meets an existing façade.
